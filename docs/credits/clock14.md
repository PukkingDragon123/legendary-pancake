# clock14: borrowed assets

The cuckoo clock's wood (`public/img/clock/wood.webp`, built by `src/onboard/tools/clockwood.py`): three Poly Haven
scans reduced to levelled luminance and recoloured at runtime through the game's own finishes (walnut, honey oak,
cherry, linden; `REAL` in `src/world/stages/cuckookit.ts`). They also drive the clock's bump maps and the aged roof
shingles.

| asset | source | author | licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural Walnut Veneer (diffuse, 2k) | https://polyhaven.com/a/natural_walnut_veneer | Jenelle van Heerden | CC0 |
| Oak Veneer 01 (diffuse, 2k) | https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01 | Jenelle van Heerden | CC0 |
| Rough Wood (diffuse, 2k) | https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood | Rob Tuytel | CC0 |

Everything else in this round (the brass patina, the office's books, spines, tooling and props in
`src/onboard/books.ts`) is painted and modelled in code.
