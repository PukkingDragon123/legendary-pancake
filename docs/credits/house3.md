# Credits: Pip's hollow oak (house, rooms, moving boxes, build mode)

The oak and its fittings are modelled for Hoot Hollow with **lowpix** (the owner's own tool, `symmetrical-goggles`):
specs in `art/lowpix/house/*.json`, generated by `art/lowpix/house/_src/*.py`, built by `art/lowpix/house/make.py`
into `public/models/house/*.glb`. Their textures are painted from the CC0 photo scans below (pulled towards the
"Moss & Moonlight" palette, shaded by painted light) plus procedural paint layers; the leaf clusters, ivy, ferns,
lettering and cobwebs are generated pixel art (`_src/leafart.py`, `_lib/hx.py`). The wallpaper and flooring are
painted at runtime from the room's finish (`src/assets/finishes.ts`).

## Techniques studied

Gacha Garage's `art/lowpix/world_arch` (spec generators in metres, photo-bashed materials, sprig-card crowns over
dark leaf cores) and BUSHED's `src/trees` (fluted trunks, root flare, alpha leaf cards) informed the oak's
construction; no code from them is copied.
