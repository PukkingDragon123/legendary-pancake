# light7 credits (realistic textures for the oak, the rooms and the ground)

Poly Haven texture scans (1k), resized, re-encoded as WebP and, where they replace an older texture, colour-matched to
its average colour so the world's palette stays as tuned. The detail packs hold the scan's normal map (red, green) and
its brightness variation (blue), see `src/gfx/surface.ts`.
