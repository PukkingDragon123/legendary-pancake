# Credits: the toy owls and their outfits (builder owl3)

## Toy library

`art/blender/lib/` (tk.py, facet.py, paint.py, face.py, fig.py, rig.py, parts.py, kit.py, _bz.py) is the owner's own
Blender toy library from **Gacha Garage** (`art/blender/toys/lib`, `sabaku/_bz.py`), copied with its comments; only its
paths were adapted. The owls, the Queen and the remade outfit pieces (`art/blender/owls/owl.py`, `outfits.py`) are
modelled and hand-painted with it for Hoot Hollow.
