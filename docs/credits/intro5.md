# Credits: the interview's answer objects, the floating pen and the pretend phone (builder intro5)

Everything this round added is made in code for Hoot Hollow; nothing new was borrowed.

- **Answer objects** (`src/onboard/props.ts`): the espresso cup and saucer with its spoon, the stoneware coffee mug,
  the rose-painted tea cup with its tea-bag tag, the "Fizz" cola can and the "ZAP!" energy can (made-up brands),
  the cocoa mug with whipped cream and marshmallows, the water glass with ice and lemon, nine cassette tapes with
  hand-written labels and little cover pictures, the "Hollow Tone" portable cassette recorder (made-up brand), the
  pillows and their pillowcases, the pillar candle on its brass chamberstick, the broadcast microphone (and its
  knitted nightcap), the brass sun, moon and sun-and-moon ornaments, a generic smartphone, the phone's little
  wooden bed, the sheep stile and the stack of bedside books with reading glasses. Turned, extruded and swept
  geometry with hand-painted pixel textures (brushwork, hue jitter, worn edges, painted labels), painted at runtime.
- **The floating fountain pen** (`src/onboard/pen.ts`): nib, feed, section, marbled barrel, posted cap, clip and
  finial, modelled and painted in code.
- **The pretend phone** (`src/ui/phone/phone.tsx`): the body, the moonlit wallpaper, the made-up apps and their
  icons (Natter, Loopreel, Scrollie, Glimmer, Questy, Mailbox, Shoppa, Newsy), the notifications, padlocks and
  the shield, painted pixel by pixel in code. No real brand, logo or app is shown.

The sheep, day bed, alarm clock, pocket watch and nightstand in the answers are the borrowed models already
credited in `intro4.md` (repacked into `public/models/interview/cards.glb`). The phone's text uses m5x7 by Daniel
Linssen and Galmuri11 (Lee Minseo, SIL Open Font License), credited there too.
