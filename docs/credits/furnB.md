# furnB credits: beds, seating, tables, storage, lights, plants and rugs

Models in `public/models/items/` for the item ids in `src/assets/decor/{beds,tables,lights,green}.ts`.

## Borrowed models

Each was taken from Gacha Garage's world props (`public/models/lowpix/world/<file>`, the user's own game, where it was
made low-poly with a hand-painted 512 px atlas from the source below), then restyled for Hoot Hollow by
`art/lowpix/items/_furnb/borrow.py` (recipe in `borrowed.py`): texture re-graded to the calm palette (less saturation,
slate shadows, lamp-gold highlights, per-item recolours), lit matte material, fitted to the item's footprint. Glowing
bulbs and flames on the two lamps are our own lowpix parts (`art/lowpix/items/_extra/`).

## Our own models

Every other id (monstera, bunk bed, canopy bed, hammock bed, basket nest, beanbag, toadstool seat, vanity, workbench, painted
dresser, library bookcase, armoire, apothecary, old-oak coffee table, fairy lights, candelabra, toadstool lamp, firefly
jar, cactus trio, trailing ivy, maple bonsai, window box, terrarium, fiddle-leaf fig and the five rugs) is modelled from
scratch as a lowpix spec (`art/lowpix/items/<id>.json`, generated by `art/lowpix/items/_furnb/*.py`), with
hand-painted textures. Every leafy plant (monstera, fiddle-leaf fig, trailing ivy, maple bonsai, geranium
window box, terrarium moss and ferns, the moss on the basket nest, the shelf plants) is built from 2D pixel-art leaf
cards that we hand-pixelled in code (`_furnb/foliage.py`). Some wood, bark, stone and leaf surfaces mix in relief from CC0 Poly Haven photo textures that
ship with lowpix (`assets/photos/`: bark_brown_02, rock_face_03, forest_leaves_02; https://polyhaven.com, CC0).
