# Audio credits

Sound effects, ambience loops and music shipped in `public/sfx/` (123 MP3 files, 91 sound ids, 10.25 MB). The first 88 files were checked on **2026-11-10**; 35 files (28 new sound ids, 6.94 MB: alarms, breathing cues, sleep soundscapes, music loops and physical-UI foley) were added and checked on **2026-11-20**.

Every file is an edit (cut, fade, gain, EQ, mono/stereo fold, loop crossfade, one pitch shift, MP3 encode) of an existing third-party recording, music track or sound-design asset. Nothing was synthesised in code and nothing was produced with an AI audio generator by this project. Machine-readable versions of this page: `public/sfx/credits.json` (per file) and `public/sfx/manifest.json` (ids the app can play).

## How licenses were verified

All checks were made on **2026-11-10** (first 88 files) and **2026-11-20** (35 added files) from the build environment, by downloading each source page and each archive and reading them directly. Nothing below relies on a search-result filter or on a third-party summary.

### OpenGameArt.org submissions (CC0)

- For every submission used, the submission page itself was downloaded and its **`License(s)`** field was read. A source was accepted only when that field lists **CC0 and nothing else**, with the label linking to `http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`. Submissions offering several licenses, or CC-BY / OGA-BY / CC-BY-SA / GPL, were not used.
- The exact field found on each page (label and link), the page's own "Copyright/Attribution Notice", the posting date, and sha256 hashes of the page copy and of the downloaded source files are recorded in `public/sfx/licenses/source-license-evidence.json`. The generator of this document refuses to run if any of those pages lists a license other than CC0.
- Where an archive ships its own license file, that file was read too and is copied verbatim into `public/sfx/licenses/`:
  - **Kenney** – *Interface Sounds* `License.txt`: "License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) … This content is free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects. Support us by crediting Kenney or www.kenney.nl (this is not mandatory)". *RPG Audio* and *Music Jingles* `license.txt`: "License (CC0) … You may use these graphics in personal and commercial projects. Credit (Kenney or www.kenney.nl) would be nice but is not mandatory."
  - **Vehicle** – *Tinysized SFX* `readme.txt`: "Distributed under Creative Commons Zero".
  - **qubodup** – *Squeaky Rat* `notes.txt`: "released under CC0. The sounds used to create these were under CC0 at the time of creation."
  - **Vehicle** – *Fantasy Accessory SFX Library* `readme.txt` (added 2026-11-20): "Distributed under Creative Commons Zero".
- The Kenney packs were taken from Kenney's own uploads on OpenGameArt (author account "Kenney"), because `kenney.nl` answered HTTP 403 to this environment. They are the same packs, with Kenney's license file inside each zip.

### U.S. National Park Service recordings (public domain)

- Eight recordings come from nps.gov: `amb_wind.mp3`, `owl_hoot.mp3`, `bee_buzz.mp3`, `frog_croak.mp3` (2026-11-10) and `alarm_birds.mp3`, `amb_brown.mp3`, `amb_waves.mp3`, `amb_forest_night.mp3` (2026-11-20). They are **public domain, not CC0**.
- Yellowstone Sound Library page (`https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/photosmultimedia/soundlibrary.htm`) states: "The files available here were recorded in the park and are in the public domain. They may be downloaded and used without limitation; however, please credit the "National Park Service" where appropriate."
- NPS Natural Sounds – Sound Gallery page (`https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm`) states: "The files are in the public domain and may be downloaded. Enjoy, and please give the appropriate credit."
- Each recording's own page was read for its credit line (wind: "NPS/Peter Comley"; owl, bee, pig frog: "NPS"). The statements and page entries are quoted in `public/sfx/licenses/nps_*_public-domain-statement.txt`.
- **Requested credit:** "National Park Service". It is a courtesy request, not a license condition; the suggested credits line above includes it.
- 2026-11-20: `alarm_birds.mp3` (Dawn Chorus, credit "NPS/Jennifer Jerrett") and `amb_brown.mp3` (Hurricane Vent, credit "NPS/Peter Comley") come from the same Yellowstone Sound Library, whose statement was re-read that day (page unchanged).
- 2026-11-20: `amb_waves.mp3` and `amb_forest_night.mp3` come from the NPS multimedia library, found through the NPS audio API (`developer.nps.gov/api/v1/multimedia/audio`). They are not on a library page with its own public-domain statement, so they were accepted only because their media records credit the NPS itself ("NPS, Kristal Ip", San Francisco Maritime NHP; "NSNSD / Emma Brown", Weir Farm NHP, NSNSD being the NPS Natural Sounds and Night Skies Division), and the NPS website disclaimer (`https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm`) states: "material created by the National Park Service and presented on this website, unless otherwise indicated, is generally considered in the public domain." The records and the disclaimer are quoted in `public/sfx/licenses/nps_multimedia-library_public-domain-statement.txt`. Recordings in that library credited to outside people (e.g. university or private recordists) were not used.

### Origin of the sounds

- Nothing in this set was generated with an AI model or synthesised in code by this project.
- The sources are a mix of field/foley recordings (footsteps, rustles, water, crunches, birds, stream, fire, crickets, owl, bee, frog, cat, coins, paper) and human-made sound design with instruments or synthesizers (Kenney's interface tones and jingles, the chimes, the win jingle, the magic and power-up sounds), as described by their authors on the source pages.
- Whether an uploader used generative tools cannot be proven from a web page. Of the 32 OpenGameArt submissions used, 27 were posted between 2010 and 2022. The five newer ones are *Chimey UI Sounds* (2024, "I decided to make my own"), *Kalimba Two-Notes* (2024, "two notes played on a kalimba"), *Various Sound Effects* (2024, "scraps from various old unfinished projects") and *Power-Up Sound Effects* (2024) by Spring Spring, and *Elevator ding* (2025, "made using some synth in Garage Band"). The four NPS files are field recordings.
- 2026-11-20 additions. Field recordings: rain (*AMB Rain Loop 2*, "Recorded on a GoPro Hero 12 Black", 2025) and the four NPS recordings. Acoustic instrument recording: the singing bowl (2008). Foley recordings: clock ticks (2011), clock winding (2020), book pages (2017), pencil, blankets, cloth flip, yawn (OwlishMedia, 2018), stationery thump (2018), drawer ("recorded on my phone", 2022), wood knocks (2018), door creak and cloth flap (Kenney, 2014), glass vials and coins ("All sounds are organic and unprocessed", 2015). Music: *Happy Lullaby* (2013), *Heavenly Loop* ("made using only ZynAddSubFX in LMMS", 2017), *Short kalimba loop* (2017), *A Small Fire Will Do* (2021), *4 Music Box Tracks* (2022, published by the author as MIDI files plus looping OGG renders; tags "music box", "celesta"). Sound design: *Chimey UI Sounds* (2024, "I decided to make my own") for `breath_in`/`breath_out`; the VCSL-derived `Ding.wav` (Robin Lamb) for `breath_hold`.
- Of the 2026-11-20 sources, two OpenGameArt submissions were posted after 2022: *Chimey UI Sounds* (2024) and *AMB Rain Loop 2* (2025, a GoPro field recording). Nothing was generated or synthesised by this project.

## Notes for maintainers

- Sounds were chosen by reading file names and descriptions and by measuring duration, envelope, pitch contour and spectrum. They were **not auditioned by ear**, so a listening pass is worth doing; `manifest.json` gains are suggestions derived from measured loudness.
- One-shots: mono, 44.1 kHz, 112 kbps MP3, leading silence trimmed, peak about -3 dBFS (`ui_type` and `voice_*` about -8 dBFS). Loops: stereo, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps MP3, about -20 LUFS, 0.5 s fade in and out (the app is expected to crossfade).
- `pet_purr.mp3` is a real cat purr with almost all of its energy below 300 Hz; it is quiet on phone speakers.
- `amb_night.mp3` is an 11.5 s seamless cricket loop repeated three times; `amb_birds.mp3` contains natural pauses between calls.
- Some slots reuse a pack sound under a different role than its file name suggests (for example `ui_error` is Kenney's `question_004`, `plant_dig` is a gravel step, `voice_d` is a short rat squeak). The table above lists every original file.
- 2026-11-20 additions: categories `alarm`, `breath`, `ambient`, `music`, `foley` and `reward` (sparkle). The new soundscapes use `ambient` as requested; the older `amb_*` ids still use `ambience`, so code that filters by category should accept both (or rename one of them).
- Loops (`alarm_*`, new `amb_*`, `music_*`) have `loop: true` and **no fade-in/out**. Soundscapes and `alarm_birds` were made seamless by crossfading the last 1-3 s of the cut into its first 1-3 s (equal-power, ffmpeg `acrossfade`); `alarm_musicbox`, `alarm_kalimba` and `alarm_bells` are cut to whole bars (4 bars) with a 60 ms crossfade; `music_*` are the authors' own loops, kept whole. MP3 adds ~25 ms of encoder delay/padding: Chrome and Firefox remove it using the LAME header written by ffmpeg, Safari may not; if a gap is audible, play the decoded buffer with `loop = true` and `loopEnd` set to the length given in the edits column, or crossfade two players.
- Loudness: soundscapes about -20 LUFS (`amb_forest_night` -22 LUFS; `amb_waves` only reaches -24.7 LUFS under the -3 dBFS peak ceiling because of wave splashes, so its manifest gain is higher, 0.55); music peaks at -6 dBFS (about -19 to -21 LUFS, `music_breath` -18 LUFS); alarms and one-shots peak at -3 dBFS. `breath_hold` is meant to be very soft (gain 0.22).
- `yawn.mp3` is a human yawn (OwlishMedia) raised 4 semitones so it reads as a small creature; the only CC0 creature yawn found (`as_d_yawn.wav` in *NHFEA: Sound*) is low and growly by measurement, so it was not used. `sparkle_1..3` are glass vials clinking (Vehicle), high-passed at 1 kHz. `wings_flutter_1/2` are cloth flaps (Kenney `cloth1`, OwlishMedia `flip`), chosen because they are softer than the existing `bird_flap`. `stamp.mp3` is an unlabelled thump from OwlishMedia's stationery set (`Stationery_17.wav`).
- `music_lullaby` (*cute tune*) and `alarm_musicbox` (*happy polka*) are different tracks from the same music-box pack, so the bedtime tune is never the wake-up tune.
